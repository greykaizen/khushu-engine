# v1.3 Zakat Design — Deferred Fiqh Expansion (design-only)

Everything below was proposed in audits/reviews and deliberately DEFERRED from
v1.3.0 code so no unverified fiqh freezes into the engine. Each item needs its
own sourced verification pass (house rule: design-on-paper-first).

## Guiding principles

1. **Methodology ≠ madhab switch.** Composable policies with presets:
   `ZakatMethodology = PRESET(HANAFI|SHAFII|MALIKI|HANBALI) | CUSTOM(policies)`.
   A user may hold a Hanafi baseline while choosing another accepted position
   on nisab/debt/investments. Every selected policy carries provenance.
2. **Facts first, rulings never.** Disputed cases return
   `RequiresScholarReview(reason)` — the livestock >120 precedent.
3. **Explainability**: every result decomposes into
   eligible/basis/nisab/hawl/rate/deductions/due/provenance/warnings — a
   "why do I owe this?" tree, not just a number.

## Deferred subsystems

### Nisab methodology
gold / silver / caller-EXPLICIT value; weights + authority per convention;
both thresholds returned with `selectionPolicy`.

### Hawl policy
continuous-ownership semantics; new acquisitions joining vs separate hawls;
lunar-vs-civil anniversary choice.

### Liabilities/debt policy (first-class)
`Liability(amount, dueSchedule, category, deductibility)` +
`DebtPolicy`. Schools genuinely differ (immediate debts, one-year installments,
long-term debt treatment — SeekersGuidance Hanafi material). Never generalize
silently.

### Asset taxonomy expansion
`Cash, BankBalance, Gold(mass,purity,valuationBasis), Silver, Jewelry,
Receivable, TradeInventory, BusinessAsset, Shares(trading vs long-term),
ETF, Fund, RetirementAccount, CryptoAsset, DigitalWallet, AgriculturalProduce,
Livestock, Rikaz, Other`. Trading vs long-term investment treatment is a real
policy split (Hanafi look-through for long holdings — SeekersGuidance).
Jewelry valuation basis (retail vs buyback) is a documented contemporary
difference.

### Valuation abstraction
`Valuation(amount, currency, valuationDate, source, confidence)` — hosts plug
prices in; engine never fetches. High-precision internal math, round only the
final payable.

### Agriculture subsystem
`AgriculturalAssessment(produce, quantity, irrigationType, mixedIrrigationPolicy,
nisabPolicy, harvestDate)` — harvest-based assessment differs structurally from
monetary hawl (Hanafi material); 10/5% irrigation distinction plus MIXED
methodology (deferred).

### Livestock extensions
grazing-status policy, trade-purpose distinction (trade animals → trade-goods
treatment), ownership/partnership rules — modeled as explicit policy fields,
never buried in algorithms. Schedule tables stay declarative data.

### Fitr expansion
due window/payment deadline facts, staple-food selection per region, cash-
equivalent representation gated by methodology.

### Recipient categories (Qur'an 9:60)
Eight `Asnaf` categories as structured FACTS with metadata. The engine never
decides who personally qualifies.

### Purification / impermissible income
`NonZakatDisposition(amount, source, treatment, requiresScholarReview)` —
informational only; distinguishes purification from zakat (contemporary Hanafi
guidance). No rulings.

### Timeline & dashboard
`zakat.timeline()` (nisabReached→hawlStart/End→assessment→due→nextAssessment),
`hawlProgress()`, `nextDueDate()`, `AnnualZakatReport`.
