# v1.3 Zakat Design — Deferred Fiqh Expansion (design-only)

Everything below was proposed in audits/reviews and deliberately DEFERRED from
v1.3.0 code so no unverified fiqh freezes into the engine. Each item needs its
own sourced verification pass (house rule: design-on-paper-first).
**Source pass 2026-08-28**: citations collected for debt, shares, agriculture,
jewelry, fitr-timing/form (see §Source pass); remaining gate is
knowledgeable-reader review, not sourcing.

## Guiding principles

1. **Methodology ≠ madhab switch.** Composable policies with presets:
   `ZakatMethodology = PRESET(HANAFI|SHAFII|MALIKI|HANBALI) | CUSTOM(policies)`.
   A user may hold a Hanafi baseline while choosing another accepted position
   on nisab/debt/investments. Every selected policy carries provenance.
2. **Facts first, rulings never.** Disputed cases return
   `RequiresScholarReview(reason)` — the livestock >120 precedent.
3. **Explainability**: every result decomposes into
   eligible/basis/nisab/hawl/rate/deductions/due/provenance/warnings — a
   "why do I owe this?" tree, not just a number.

## Deferred subsystems

### Nisab methodology
gold / silver / caller-EXPLICIT value; weights + authority per convention;
both thresholds returned with `selectionPolicy`.

### Hawl policy
continuous-ownership semantics; new acquisitions joining vs separate hawls;
lunar-vs-civil anniversary choice.

### Liabilities/debt policy (first-class)
`Liability(amount, dueSchedule, category, deductibility)` +
`DebtPolicy`. Schools genuinely differ (immediate debts, one-year installments,
long-term debt treatment — SeekersGuidance Hanafi material). Never generalize
silently.

### Asset taxonomy expansion
`Cash, BankBalance, Gold(mass,purity,valuationBasis), Silver, Jewelry,
Receivable, TradeInventory, BusinessAsset, Shares(trading vs long-term),
ETF, Fund, RetirementAccount, CryptoAsset, DigitalWallet, AgriculturalProduce,
Livestock, Rikaz, Other`. Trading vs long-term investment treatment is a real
policy split (Hanafi look-through for long holdings — SeekersGuidance).
Jewelry valuation basis (retail vs buyback) is a documented contemporary
difference.

### Valuation abstraction
`Valuation(amount, currency, valuationDate, source, confidence)` — hosts plug
prices in; engine never fetches. High-precision internal math, round only the
final payable.

### Agriculture subsystem
`AgriculturalAssessment(produce, quantity, irrigationType, mixedIrrigationPolicy,
nisabPolicy, harvestDate)` — harvest-based assessment differs structurally from
monetary hawl (Hanafi material); 10/5% irrigation distinction plus MIXED
methodology (deferred).

### Livestock extensions
grazing-status policy, trade-purpose distinction (trade animals → trade-goods
treatment), ownership/partnership rules — modeled as explicit policy fields,
never buried in algorithms. Schedule tables stay declarative data.

### Fitr expansion
due window/payment deadline facts, staple-food selection per region, cash-
equivalent representation gated by methodology.

### Recipient categories (Qur'an 9:60)
Eight `Asnaf` categories as structured FACTS with metadata. The engine never
decides who personally qualifies.

### Purification / impermissible income
`NonZakatDisposition(amount, source, treatment, requiresScholarReview)` —
informational only; distinguishes purification from zakat (contemporary Hanafi
guidance). No rulings.

### Timeline & dashboard
`zakat.timeline()` (nisabReached→hawlStart/End→assessment→due→nextAssessment),
`hawlProgress()`, `nextDueDate()`, `AnnualZakatReport`.

## Source pass (2026-08-28, web-collected — pending human review)

### Liabilities/debt policy
Four-madhah comparison (mizaan.ca/blog/zakat-on-debt, cross-checked with
*The Five Schools of Islamic Law*, al-islam.org):
- **Hanafi**: debts deductible from zakatable wealth (broadest). Contemp.
  scholars split on long-term debt: full balance vs. current-year payments
  only (leaning: current year).
- **Shafi'i**: NO deduction — zakat on gross wealth ("freedom from debt is
  not a condition" — al-islam.org).
- **Maliki**: short-term (due-this-year) debts only.
- **Hanbali**: deductible, broadly Hanafi-like.
- Receivables: dayn qawi (strong, expected repayment) included; dayn da'if
  excluded until collected; retro-assessment on collection is a live
  scholarly split → `RequiresScholarReview` territory.

### Shares/investments
- AAOIFI methodology (Sharia Standards, aaoifi.com): **trading portfolio =
  2.5% of full market value**; **long-term investment = 2.5% of the
  zakatable-asset proportion of the company** (look-through / liquid-assets
  method), dividends included. Secondary summaries: halalrates.com,
  zakatable.com (also cites the FCNA zakatable-portion method).
- Qaradawi nuance (zakatview.com/library/zakat-on-investments): passive
  long-term holders with no management interest treated closer to traders —
  record as a distinct policy option, not a default.

### Agriculture
- **Rate hadith**: Sahih al-Bukhari 1483 — "On land irrigated by rain water
  or natural water channels … one-tenth; on land irrigated by the well, half
  of an ushr" (sunnah.com/bukhari:1483).
- **Nisab hadith (5 awsuq)**: Muslim (sunnah.com/urn/406140): "whoever
  harvests five awsuq of dates, or picks five awsuq of grapes, or reaps five
  awsuq of wheat, has to pay zakat …"; also the agreed-upon "no zakat below
  five awsuq".
- **IIFA resolution** (iifa-aifi.org/en/32838.html): 10% natural irrigation
  (rain/rivers), 5% where irrigation costs are incurred.
- **Mixed irrigation**: dominant-method rule; equal split → 7.5% per several
  contemporary summaries (islamqa.info #100188, myzakatcalc.com) — exact
  classical weighting to verify at implementation.
- Harvest-time assessment (not hawl-based) — islamweb.net #139046.

### Jewelry
- **Hanafi**: zakat due on ALL gold/silver jewelry worn or not (zakatestimator.com
  4-madhah comparison; islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam #985).
- **Maliki/Shafi'i/Hanbali**: worn personal-use jewelry within custom
  exempt; zakat if held as investment/exceeds custom (rafiq.money,
  zakatable.com; Maliki "settled position" per rafiq).

### Fitr expansion
- **Cash vs food**: Hanafi permits cash value — Darul Uloom Trinidad #52548,
  Egypt Dar al-Ifta #3528; early precedent: 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz, Hasan
  al-Basri (SeekersGuidance). Maliki/Shafi'i/Hanbali require food (NZF
  Canada fiqhi analysis).
- **Due-time split**: Hanafi = dawn of Eid day; Shafi'i/Hanbali = sunset
  ending Ramadan (misrconnect summary); Sunnah window = 1–2 days before Eid
  (AMJA #87752 citing Bukhari from Ibn 'Umar; Muwatta narration).
- Amount: one sa' of staple food — hadith Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, Sahihain
  (masarat-sy.org four-schools summary).
